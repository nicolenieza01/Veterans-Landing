Before you start

It takes about ten minutes. Have these handy and you can finish in one sitting.

  • Contact details for your current and previous landlord
  • Your income sources and someone who can verify them
  • An emergency contact
  • Vehicle details, if you park with us

Nothing is sent until you press submit at the end. If you get stuck, call the leasing office and we will walk through it with you.

Rental application

Submission does not guarantee approval or reserve a unit. Nothing is sent until you press submit at the bottom.

1Applicant information

Write N/A if an item does not apply. Do not provide medical records or details about a disability on this form.

2Co-applicants and household members

List every person who will live in the unit. Each adult may be required to complete screening authorization.

3Current and prior housing
4Income and rental assistance

List all lawful income you want considered: employment, self-employment, pension, Social Security, SSI or SSDI, VA benefits, child support, alimony, or other verifiable sources.

5Vehicles, pets, and service or assistance animals

A request for a reasonable accommodation may be made separately. Do not state a diagnosis or provide medical details here. Service and assistance animals are not treated as pets where applicable law requires.

6Rental history and screening disclosures

A Yes answer does not automatically result in denial. Veterans Landing evaluates information under its written screening standards and applicable law.

Has any landlord filed an eviction action against you?
Do you currently owe money to a landlord or utility provider?
Have you materially violated a lease or rental agreement?
Have you been asked to leave a rental property for reasons other than ordinary lease expiration?
Have you used another name on a prior lease, utility account, employment record, or public record?

8Optional veteran program coordination
